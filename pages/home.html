Research Opportunities

My research focuses on social influence, recommender systems, reinforcement learning, and agent-based modeling. My current work explores the following directions:

  • Reinforcement learning for influence maximization and recommendation
  • Multimodal, social, and session-based recommendation
  • Agent-based modeling of influence diffusion
  • Incentive allocation in social networks

I am always looking for self-motivated students interested in these directions. If you are interested, please feel free to send me your CV by email.

Bio

Dr. Shiqing Wu is an Assistant Professor of Artificial Intelligence in the Faculty of Data Science at City University of Macau, Macau SAR, China. He was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the School of Computer Science at the University of Technology Sydney, Australia, from 2022 to 2024. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Tasmania, Australia, in 2022, and a joint B.Sc. degree in Computer Science from Auckland University of Technology, New Zealand, and China Jiliang University, China, in 2016.

Shiqing's research interests include agent-based modeling, social influence analysis, influence maximization, recommender systems, and reinforcement learning. His research works have been published in prestigious venues, including conferences (e.g., KDD, SIGIR, ICDM, IJCAI, ACM MM, AAMAS, etc.) and journals (e.g., IEEE TKDE, IEEE TCSS, IEEE TAI, ACM TOIS, Information Fusion, Information Sciences, etc.). Shiqing also serves as a regular Program Committee Member for conferences such as AAAI, NIPS, IJCAI, CVPR, SIGIR, KDD, ACM Multimedia, ECAI, and PRICAI. He is an Editorial Board Member of Information Processing & Management.
